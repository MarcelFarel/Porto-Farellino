Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Cryptocurrency? +
Cryptocurrency is a digital or virtual currency that is secured by cryptography, making it nearly impossible to counterfeit or double-spend. It operates independently of a central bank and uses decentralized technology called blockchain to record transactions across multiple computers.
How do I buy Cryptocurrency? +
To buy cryptocurrency, you need to: 1) Create an account on a trusted exchange like cryPIto, 2) Complete identity verification, 3) Add a payment method (bank account or card), 4) Choose your cryptocurrency, 5) Place your buy order, and 6) Store your crypto in a secure wallet.
Is Cryptocurrency safe? +
Cryptocurrency can be safe when proper security measures are followed. The blockchain technology itself is very secure, but risks include exchange hacks, wallet security, and market volatility. Always use reputable platforms, enable two-factor authentication, and never share your private keys.
What is blockchain technology? +
Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that maintains a continuously growing list of records (blocks) linked and secured using cryptography. Each block contains a timestamp and link to the previous block, making it resistant to modification and providing transparency and security.

Beginner's Guide

  1. Create an account on a trusted platform (like cryPIto) Choose a reputable cryptocurrency exchange that offers security features, user-friendly interface, and good customer support.
  2. Get a wallet (custodial or non-custodial) Decide between a custodial wallet (managed by exchange) or non-custodial wallet (you control private keys) based on your security preferences.
  3. Learn about different coins (start with Bitcoin or Ethereum) Research the top cryptocurrencies, understand their use cases, technology, and market position before investing.
  4. Start small — buy, hold, observe market trends Begin with small amounts to learn the market dynamics, practice trading, and understand price volatility.

Tips & Tricks

✓ Do's:

  • Do Your Own Research (DYOR): Always understand the project before investing
  • Use Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for better security
  • Diversify Your Portfolio: Don't put all your money in one coin
  • Follow Market News: Stay updated with the crypto world
  • Practice Patience: Crypto is volatile — think long term

✗ Don'ts:

  • Don't Share Your Private Key: Ever
  • Don't Fall for Scams or Promises of Guaranteed Returns
  • Don't Invest More Than You Can Afford to Lose
  • Don't Panic Sell during market drops

Portfolio Management

  • Set clear investment goals and risk tolerance
  • Use dollar-cost averaging for regular investments
  • Regularly review and rebalance your portfolio
  • Keep detailed records for tax purposes
